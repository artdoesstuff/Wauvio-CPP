Compose engine (wauvio/compose)

MIDI export, mastering and the example

See it in action: The Composer

MIDI export — compose/midi.hpp

save_midi(song, path, ppq = 480) writes the song as a Standard MIDI File: a tempo track, then one track per arrangement track, with names. The first four tracks use channels 0–3 with General MIDI programs piano (0), 4 (electric piano), 33 (fingered bass) and 48 (string ensemble); tracks five to eight go to channel 9 (the drum channel). Rests are skipped; chords become simultaneous notes. The instruments you used are not stored, so a MIDI player will use its own sounds, or use Wauvio’s MIDI layer.

Mastered render — compose/fx.hpp

  • render_mastered(song, MasterSettings, MasterReport* report = nullptr, RenderOptions) — builds a mixer from the arrangement, applies mastering and returns the stereo buffer.
  • save_mastered(song, wav_path, MasterSettings, RenderOptions) — the same, written to WAV with the level preserved (not renormalised).
  • Pair with fx::master_for_genre("jazz") to get genre-appropriate loudness and tone.

These two headers are separate because they pull in fx and midi, which many compose users do not need.

Example program

wauvio/compose/examples/compose.cpp builds a quick draft, a full jazz song with MIDI export and mastering, and a song from examples.wcp; build it with g++ -std=c++17 -O2 compose.cpp -o compose from that folder.
